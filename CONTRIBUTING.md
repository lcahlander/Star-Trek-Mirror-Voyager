# Contributing to Mirror Voyager

Welcome, fellow fans! Open a GitHub Issue to propose a character arc, continuity correction, or episode idea. Please keep suggestions consistent with the [story guide](docs/STORY_GUIDE.md), especially B’Elanna and Chakotay’s Maquis background and the Caretaker sabotage.

Be constructive and respectful. Clearly label fan speculation and original ideas; do not present them as franchise canon. Do not upload copyrighted TV footage, official music, or third-party art without permission.

To suggest changes, open an Issue or propose a Pull Request. The repository owner decides what becomes part of this fan continuity.
