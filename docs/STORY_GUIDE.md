# Star Trek: Mirror Voyager — Story Guide

The Terran Empire • An unofficial fan-created story guide

One warship. Two rival factions. Seventy thousand light-years from home.

# The premise

## A warship far from its emperor

In 2371, the ISS Voyager pursues Chakotay’s Maquis raider into the Badlands. The Caretaker transports both ships to the Delta Quadrant, 70,000 light-years from Imperial space. Captain Kathryn Janeway sees the array as a weapon capable of transforming the Terran Empire. Chakotay authorizes B’Elanna Torres to sabotage it; Tuvok covertly supplies intelligence. The unstable array is destroyed, stranding the survivors.

## A forced alliance

Janeway accepts the surviving Maquis aboard Voyager because she needs their skills and Chakotay’s influence. The crew is divided between Imperial loyalists, revolutionaries, opportunists, and individuals struggling to discover a conscience. The ship’s mission is officially to return home; privately, every faction has a different plan for what happens when it arrives.

## The central conflict

Janeway wants to return powerful enough to challenge the Emperor. Chakotay and B’Elanna want to end Terran oppression. Tuvok believes the voyage may prove that cooperation is stronger than imperial coercion. Their survival depends on a fragile alliance that could collapse at any moment.

# The crew

## Captain Kathryn Janeway — Command

A brilliant, ruthless Imperial captain who intends to use Delta Quadrant technology to seize the throne. Her encounters with independent societies gradually challenge her belief that power alone confers legitimacy.

## Commander Chakotay — First Officer

A former Imperial officer and Maquis resistance leader. He authorized sabotage of the Caretaker array and now quietly builds a network aboard Voyager to protect his people and ultimately overthrow the Empire.

## B’Elanna Torres — Chief Engineer

A former Maquis saboteur, not an Academy-trained loyalist. Her mother resisted Terran occupation; her father betrayed the resistance. She secretly develops propulsion technology to bring Voyager home as a weapon of liberation.

## Commander Tuvok — Security and Tactical

A Vulcan intelligence operative who infiltrated the Maquis before the Badlands pursuit. He quietly helped enable the array sabotage and studies whether this divided crew can build a society without an emperor.

## Seven of Nine — Borg Specialist

A liberated Borg drone who believes her technological superiority entitles her to shape the future. Her rivalry with B’Elanna pits centralized control against rebellious innovation.

## Tom Paris — Helmsman

A disgraced Imperial pilot, son of Admiral Owen Paris, who betrayed a Maquis operation and was imprisoned. He remains a charming opportunist, torn between survival, B’Elanna, and a complicated family legacy.

## Ensign Harry Kim — Operations

An intelligence prodigy who deliberately avoids promotion. His low rank lets him remain underestimated while he accumulates access, secrets, and leverage over every faction aboard Voyager.

## The Doctor — Chief Medical Officer

Originally an Imperial hologram designed for battlefield treatment and coercive medical procedures. B’Elanna removes command restrictions; he develops a medical conscience and increasingly refuses unethical orders.

## Neelix — Procurement and Intelligence

A cheerful Talaxian trader and black-market broker who builds a network of contacts along Voyager’s route. His friendly mess hall is also a clearinghouse for information.

## Kes — Psionic Adviser

An Ocampa survivor whose growing telepathy and visions reveal competing futures. She fears that returning Janeway to Earth too soon could unleash conquest across the galaxy.

## Ensign Lon Suder — Engineering Support

A former Maquis operative with violent impulses who deliberately chooses restraint. Tuvok helps him develop a moral code; aboard this ship, Suder’s refusal to kill becomes an act of rebellion.

# Episodes and turning points

## The Caretaker — A conquest denied

Janeway tries to capture the Caretaker’s array rather than destroy it for humanitarian reasons. Maquis sabotage spirals out of control, leaving Voyager stranded and forcing an alliance between enemies.

## The 37’s — A world without an emperor

Voyager discovers Amelia Earhart and the descendants of humans abducted from 1930s Earth. Janeway attempts to impose Imperial sovereignty on their free colony, but resistance within her crew forces her to negotiate a trade agreement instead. Earhart gives her a compass and an uncomfortable lesson about leadership.

## Equinox — The Emperor’s Mercy

Voyager encounters Captain Rudolph Ransom and the ISS Equinox. Ransom has exploited sentient extradimensional beings for propulsion. Janeway defeats his attempted takeover and orders her crew to seek another way to reproduce the technology.

## The hidden war aboard Voyager

Chakotay organizes a resistance network; B’Elanna secretly develops a propulsion breakthrough; Harry gathers everyone’s secrets; Tuvok surveils the factions; Kes sees futures in which the ship destroys itself. Even the mess hall is an intelligence battleground.

# The return and the Delta Quadrant War

## Homecoming

Voyager returns to Earth carrying Borg-derived systems, advanced propulsion research, and intelligence on hundreds of worlds. The Emperor orders Janeway to surrender the ship and its discoveries. She delays, knowing the Admiralty sees her as both an asset and a threat.

## Operation Dominion

The Empire prepares a conquest of the Delta Quadrant through transwarp corridors and expeditionary fleets. Janeway is appointed to lead the campaign—a promotion that is also a trap. Chakotay and B’Elanna organize opposition, while Seven warns that attacking Borg infrastructure could expose Earth to assimilation.

## The second stranding

Janeway turns against the Emperor’s strategy when Imperial aggression threatens to unleash the Borg toward the Alpha Quadrant. Voyager’s coalition destroys a critical transwarp connection, cutting off the invasion route but stranding an Imperial fleet in the Delta Quadrant.

## An uncertain future

With the Emperor dead and Imperial command fractured, Janeway must choose: return to seize the throne, establish her own empire, or defend an alliance of free worlds. Her crew doubts she can resist the temptation to rule.

# Story principles and future possibilities

## What is established in this fan continuity

B’Elanna and Chakotay remain former Maquis. Tuvok infiltrated the Maquis. Janeway never voluntarily destroys the Caretaker array to protect the Ocampa. The crew’s internal ideological conflict is as important as the external threats. Harry’s perpetual ensign status is intentional.

## Open stories to explore

How does the Mirror crew encounter the Hirogen, Vidiians, Krenim, Species 8472, and the Borg Queen? What happens if the Doctor’s personhood becomes public in Imperial space? Can Kes prevent the future she fears without becoming another manipulator? Does Janeway change—or merely learn to disguise her ambition?

## A note to readers

This is an unofficial, fan-created alternate-universe interpretation inspired by Star Trek: Voyager. It is not canonical or affiliated with the franchise rights holders. The stories and character arcs are original fan-fiction concepts developed collaboratively in conversation.

# New continuity: House of Varek

B’Elanna Torres is a daughter of [House Varek](../houses/house-of-varek.md) through her mother Miral. The house is a sworn ally of the House of Martok and fights Terran occupation. Its expertise in engineering and sabotage informs B’Elanna’s Maquis career.
