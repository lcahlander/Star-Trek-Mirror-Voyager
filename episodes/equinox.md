# Equinox — The Emperor’s Mercy

**Premise:** Ransom’s ISS Equinox uses sentient beings as propulsion fuel.

## Synopsis

Captain Rudolph Ransom of the ISS Equinox has killed sentient extradimensional beings to power an experimental drive. Janeway rejects Ransom’s attempted takeover, and the Doctor and B’Elanna push for an ethical alternative.
