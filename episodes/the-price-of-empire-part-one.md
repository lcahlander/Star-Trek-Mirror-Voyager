# STAR TREK: MIRROR VOYAGER
## “THE PRICE OF EMPIRE”
### Part One — Extended Teleplay Draft

**Unofficial, noncommercial fan fiction.** An alternate-universe reimagining, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Star Trek rights holders.

**Format:** Extended five-act teleplay draft for a target 45-minute episode. Actual runtime depends on performance, editing, and effects; not a timed shooting script.  
**Setting:** Terran Empire, 2371; the Badlands; the Delta Quadrant.  
**Continuity:** The ISS Voyager pursues Chakotay's Maquis ship. Tuvok is an undercover Imperial officer who covertly protects the resistance. Janeway attempts to capture the Caretaker's array. Chakotay authorizes B'Elanna to disable it; Tuvok supplies critical intelligence. A cascade destroys the array and strands Voyager. The Maquis are integrated by necessity, not forgiveness.

# PART ONE — THE HUNT

## TEASER

### EXT. BADLANDS — SPACE

Plasma storms blossom across the darkness like bruises made of fire. A battered MAQUIS RAIDER tumbles through the eddies, its starboard nacelle trailing sparks.

Behind it, an Imperial patrol ship struggles to follow.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — BRIDGE

The bridge is cramped, hot, and crowded with improvised controls. CHAKOTAY sits at the helm, his tattoo stark under red emergency lighting. B'ELANNA TORRES stands at engineering. TUVOK, wearing plain civilian clothing, monitors tactical sensors. LON SUDER works a secondary console.

**B'ELANNA**  
The port plasma injector is going to shear off if you do that again.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Then I'd better not do it again.

The ship bucks.

**SUDER**  
Imperial patrol closing. Their weapons are charging.

**B'ELANNA**  
I can overload their targeting array. It will burn out our secondary relay.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Do it.

**TUVOK**  
An alternative exists. The plasma turbulence at bearing zero-three-four will interfere with their sensors for eleven seconds.

**CHAKOTAY**  
You sound very certain.

**TUVOK**  
I have studied their tracking doctrine.

Chakotay studies him, then turns the ship sharply. A wave of plasma swallows the raider. The Imperial ship fires into empty space.

**B'ELANNA**  
Eleven seconds? You'd better be right.

**TUVOK**  
I generally am.

Suder watches a damage report scroll by. He notices a red indicator: *IMPERIAL TRANSPONDER DETECTED — ENCRYPTED*. He silently erases it from the shared display, but not from his own memory.

### EXT. BADLANDS — SPACE

The raider vanishes deeper into the storm.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**TITLE: STAR TREK: MIRROR VOYAGER**

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — MEDICAL ALCOVE — EARLIER

A converted storage compartment serves as a makeshift infirmary. A wounded young Maquis courier, INA, lies under a thermal blanket. SUDER holds a pressure dressing against her shoulder. His hands are remarkably steady.

**INA**  
You could have left me at the relay station.

**SUDER**  
Yes.

**INA**  
Why didn't you?

Suder considers this with the care another person might give a difficult calculation.

**SUDER**  
Chakotay ordered me to bring everyone back.

**INA**  
Is that the only reason?

He does not answer. Chakotay enters with a field medical kit.

**CHAKOTAY**  
The medics on Tandar said she shouldn't travel.

**SUDER**  
Then the medics should have kept the Empire from bombing their hospital.

Chakotay kneels beside Ina. The raider shudders around them.

**CHAKOTAY**  
You stay here until we reach the next safe harbor. That's an order.

**INA**  
You can't order me not to be afraid.

**CHAKOTAY**  
No. But I can promise we won't abandon you.

Suder watches Chakotay make the promise. It is a strange luxury, a promise given when there is no guarantee it can be kept.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — SECURITY OFFICE — SAME DAY

An Imperial intelligence officer, LIEUTENANT VAREK, reviews the Maquis dossier with Commander Cavit. Photographs flicker across a wall display: Chakotay, B'Elanna, Suder, a blurred image of Tuvok.

**VAREK**  
Torres designed the explosives that disabled the Kestrel shipyard. She also evacuated the civilian maintenance crews.

**CAVIT**  
An inconsistency.

**VAREK**  
A vulnerability. People who value lives can be made to choose between them.

Cavit points at Suder's file.

**CAVIT**  
And him?

**VAREK**  
No such vulnerability. Yet Chakotay took him off combat duty after an unauthorized execution. Their commander may be sentimental.

**CAVIT**  
Our captain is not.

Varek closes the dossier.

**VAREK**  
That is why I'm more worried about the captain than the rebels.


## ACT ONE

### INT. IMPERIAL DETENTION FACILITY — INTERROGATION ROOM

TOM PARIS sits behind a steel table, bruised but smirking. His uniform insignia has been torn away. The door opens. CAPTAIN KATHRYN JANEWAY enters in black and crimson, followed by a security guard.

**PARIS**  
If you're here to shoot me, I'd appreciate it if you did it before lunch.

**JANEWAY**  
Lieutenant Thomas Eugene Paris. Disgraced Imperial pilot. Convicted of falsifying a combat report. Briefly employed by the Maquis. Informant for Imperial Intelligence. And somehow still alive.

**PARIS**  
You left out charming.

Janeway lays a PADD on the table: a map of the Badlands.

**JANEWAY**  
Chakotay is hiding in these plasma storms. You're going to help me find him.

**PARIS**  
I helped the Empire once. Look where it got me.

**JANEWAY**  
You were promised a pardon. I'm promising something more useful: a ship to fly.

**PARIS**  
And if I decline?

Janeway turns to the guard.

**JANEWAY**  
Return him to solitary. Suspend his oxygen allotment for twenty seconds every hour until he changes his mind.

**PARIS**  
All right. I admire a woman who negotiates.

Janeway sits, close enough that Paris stops smiling.

**JANEWAY**  
You betrayed the Maquis once. Do not make me regret trusting you to do it again.

### EXT. IMPERIAL SHIPYARD — ORBIT

The ISS VOYAGER hangs among construction platforms: a sleek, heavily armed Intrepid-class warship with dark hull panels, red running lights, and the Terran globe-and-sword insignia.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIDGE

Crew stand rigidly at attention. ENSIGN HARRY KIM, fresh from the Academy, occupies Operations. A senior tactical officer runs security while the absent Tuvok remains assigned to classified duty.

Janeway strides onto the bridge. COMMANDER CAVIT rises from the first officer's chair.

**CAVIT**  
Captain on the bridge.

**JANEWAY**  
Status.

**CAVIT**  
Weapons ready. Warp engines at full capability. The Admiralty expects Chakotay's head within seventy-two hours.

**JANEWAY**  
Then the Admiralty has underestimated how long I intend to question him.

She sees Kim.

**JANEWAY**  
Ensign. Your first assignment?

**KIM**  
Yes, Captain.

**JANEWAY**  
A useful opportunity to learn what happens when people disappoint me.

**KIM**  
I'll remember that.

**JANEWAY**  
See that you do.

Paris enters, now in a provisional uniform. Cavit grimaces.

**CAVIT**  
You brought a convicted traitor onto my bridge.

**JANEWAY**  
I brought a pilot who knows the Badlands. If you object, Commander, submit a report. I'll read it after the mission.

Paris takes the helm.

**PARIS**  
Nice ship. Does she handle as well as she looks?

**JANEWAY**  
You'll have an opportunity to demonstrate that.

**PARIS**  
I was afraid you'd say that.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — ENGINEERING

B'Elanna opens a maintenance panel, exposing a captured Imperial power regulator.

**B'ELANNA**  
If we can steal one more regulator, I can give us an extra ten percent at impulse.

Chakotay enters.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Our supply contact is dead. Imperial Security raided the rendezvous point.

**B'ELANNA**  
Someone talked.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Or someone listened. Keep the crew together.

**B'ELANNA**  
What about Suder?

**CHAKOTAY**  
He stays off weapons. That's not negotiable.

**B'ELANNA**  
He saved us at Tandar.

**CHAKOTAY**  
He killed a prisoner who had surrendered. We don't become the Empire just because we're fighting it.

She looks away. That argument still hurts because she knows he's right.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — OFFICERS' MESS — NIGHT

The ship's mess is polished, quiet, and watched by a security camera. Harry Kim eats alone, studying a technical manual on a PADD. Tom Paris drops into the opposite chair without asking.

**PARIS**  
You read during dinner?

**KIM**  
The captain expects the ship's systems to be operational.

**PARIS**  
They are operational.

**KIM**  
Not all of them are properly documented.

**PARIS**  
Congratulations. You've discovered the Empire.

Harry glances toward the camera.

**KIM**  
Doesn't that bother you?

**PARIS**  
Everything bothers me. I've learned to be selective.

A junior officer walks past. Paris waits until the officer is gone.

**PARIS**  
A word of advice, Ensign: never let anyone know how much you understand. The Empire promotes people it can control and disposes of people it can't.

**KIM**  
Is that why you lost your rank?

**PARIS**  
No. I lost my rank because I thought I was smarter than everyone else.

**KIM**  
Were you?

Paris grins, but his eyes remain tired.

**PARIS**  
Not everyone.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — STORAGE BAY — NIGHT

B'Elanna sorts stolen power cells while Tuvok quietly inventories medical supplies. The compartment's door is sealed.

**B'ELANNA**  
You know Imperial equipment too well.

**TUVOK**  
The occupation has provided extensive opportunities for study.

**B'ELANNA**  
That's not an answer.

**TUVOK**  
It is an accurate statement.

She turns a regulator over in her hands.

**B'ELANNA**  
My father made regulators like this. He worked for them. When my mother was arrested, he told me there was nothing he could do.

**TUVOK**  
Do you believe he was correct?

**B'ELANNA**  
I believe he wanted to be.

A long silence. Tuvok sets down a medical pack.

**TUVOK**  
It is possible to work within an oppressive system without accepting its objectives.

**B'ELANNA**  
Spoken like someone who has tried.

Tuvok meets her eyes. For a moment, the truth is almost visible.

**TUVOK**  
Spoken like someone who has observed.



### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIDGE — DEPARTURE CHECK

The ship hums with the controlled violence of a warship waiting for permission to move. At Operations, Harry Kim is trying to reconcile two orders that disagree about the same prisoner.

**KIM**  
Commander Cavit, the transfer manifest lists Paris as a civilian adviser. Security has him under armed guard. Which designation takes priority?

**CAVIT**  
Whichever prevents him from reaching the helm without my permission.

**KIM**  
That isn't an available field.

**CAVIT**  
Then invent one.

Harry creates a field. Cavit watches the screen for a moment, surprised by how quickly it appears.

**CAVIT**  
You've done that before.

**KIM**  
No, sir. But the system was designed by people who assumed nobody would ask.

Janeway enters unnoticed at the rear of the bridge.

**JANEWAY**  
An interesting observation, Ensign. Keep it to yourself until it becomes useful.

Cavit stiffens. Janeway gestures to the viewscreen, where the shipyard recedes against a red planet.

**JANEWAY**  
We have a mission. I don't intend to return with excuses or a debris field. I intend to return with the Maquis command structure intact.

**CAVIT**  
The Emperor's warrant specifies termination.

**JANEWAY**  
The warrant specifies success. The method is my concern.

She takes the command chair. Harry files the contradictory orders in a private, encrypted directory. He gives it an innocuous name: *Training Exercises*.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — GUEST QUARTERS — LATER

Paris studies the luxury of a room that locks from the outside. A security guard stands in the doorway. Janeway enters carrying a padd.

**PARIS**  
You know, most captains at least pretend this is an invitation.

**JANEWAY**  
Your father requested that you be treated as an officer.

**PARIS**  
And you refused?

**JANEWAY**  
I told him I would treat you as an asset. That was generous.

She places the padd on a table: an image of Chakotay's raider, a list of names, and a photograph of B'Elanna.

**JANEWAY**  
Tell me what Chakotay will do when he sees a patrol ship on his port quarter.

**PARIS**  
Turn starboard. He'll expect the port ship to force him into the plasma current.

**JANEWAY**  
And if we place Voyager there?

**PARIS**  
He'll recognize the trap. He isn't stupid.

**JANEWAY**  
Neither am I. That's why you're here.

Paris studies B'Elanna's image. His usual grin fades.

**PARIS**  
If you capture them, what happens to her?

**JANEWAY**  
That depends on how useful she is. You should understand the principle.

She leaves. Paris turns the padd face down, but he cannot make himself stop looking at it.


## ACT TWO

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — READY ROOM

Janeway appears on a secure holographic channel with ADMIRAL OWEN PARIS.

**OWEN PARIS**  
I understand you've taken my son aboard.

**JANEWAY**  
Your son is an excellent pilot and a disappointing officer. I intend to make use of the first quality.

**OWEN PARIS**  
The Maquis cell has penetrated sensitive installations. I want their intelligence network intact.

**JANEWAY**  
Then you should have assigned me a capture mission, Admiral, not an extermination order.

**OWEN PARIS**  
You have your orders.

The transmission ends. Janeway studies a private file marked *MAQUIS INFILTRATION — TUVOK*. She deletes the visible copy from her desktop but leaves a hidden encrypted archive.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIDGE

**KIM**  
Captain, we're receiving a narrow-band transmission from the raider. Encryption matches an Imperial Intelligence cipher.

Janeway's eyes narrow.

**JANEWAY**  
Route it to my console. No recording in the ship's log.

Harry obeys. Before deleting the relay buffer, he saves an encrypted checksum of the transmission to a private diagnostic partition. A small, deliberate act.

**PARIS**  
Badlands in five minutes. Once we enter, you can't fly by the book.

**CAVIT**  
Then perhaps you should refrain from flying at all.

**PARIS**  
I'll keep that in mind when we're not on fire.

### EXT. BADLANDS — SPACE

Voyager plunges into the storms. The Maquis raider appears ahead.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — BRIDGE

**SUDER**  
Intrepid-class. Imperial transponder. ISS Voyager.

B'Elanna turns pale with fury.

**B'ELANNA**  
Janeway.

**CHAKOTAY**  
You know her?

**B'ELANNA**  
I know what she did to the shipyards at Kessik.

**TUVOK**  
Her tactical record suggests she will attempt to disable rather than destroy us.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Why?

**TUVOK**  
She wants prisoners.

**B'ELANNA**  
She can have our wreckage.

A vast energy wave flashes through the storm, swallowing both ships.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIDGE

Consoles erupt. Officers are thrown from their stations. Cavit falls against a shattered rail. Paris fights the helm.

**PARIS**  
That's not a plasma storm!

**KIM**  
Spatial displacement! Range exceeds the sensors!

**JANEWAY**  
Hold the ship together!

White light.

### EXT. DELTA QUADRANT — SPACE

Voyager appears in an unfamiliar starfield. Nearby, a colossal alien array turns silently. The Maquis raider emerges behind them, badly damaged.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIDGE

Emergency lighting. Smoke. Cavit lies motionless. Janeway checks his pulse, then rises without ceremony.

**JANEWAY**  
Medical report.

**KIM**  
Sickbay's been hit. The chief surgeon is dead. Multiple casualties throughout the ship.

**JANEWAY**  
Activate the Emergency Medical Hologram. Helm, position us between the raider and that structure.

**PARIS**  
Captain, we're seventy thousand light-years from home.

Silence.

**JANEWAY**  
Then our mission has changed.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — CASUALTY STATION — DELTA QUADRANT

Emergency lighting paints the deck red. The organic medical team lies beneath blankets. Harry helps a technician carry an injured security officer onto a bed. The EMH materializes, annoyed by the chaos.

**DOCTOR**  
State the nature of the medical emergency.

**KIM**  
Multiple casualties, decompression injuries, possible radiation exposure.

**DOCTOR**  
A pleasingly concise report. Who is in command of this facility?

Harry looks toward the covered bodies.

**KIM**  
You are.

The Doctor looks at the bodies, then at the patients.

**DOCTOR**  
How inconvenient for everyone. You—apply pressure there. Not on the fracture. Unless you intend to create a second emergency.

A prisoner in an Imperial holding collar is brought in with a severe burn. A guard blocks the way.

**GUARD**  
This one can wait. He's not crew.

**DOCTOR**  
He is bleeding on my floor. That makes him my patient. Move.

**GUARD**  
His clearance—

**DOCTOR**  
Has no relevance to the location of his arteries.

The guard steps aside. Harry notices the Doctor's casual defiance of protocol.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — OBSERVATION LOUNGE — LATER

Janeway studies a projection of the Caretaker array. Cavit, Harry, Paris, and a security officer stand across from her. The starfield beyond the windows is unfamiliar.

**CAVIT**  
Our standing orders concern Maquis suppression, Captain. We should locate a return route and report to High Command.

**JANEWAY**  
Seventy thousand light-years is not a return route. It's a strategic advantage, if we know how to exploit it.

**PARIS**  
Or an excellent place to die unnoticed.

Janeway taps the array diagram.

**JANEWAY**  
This mechanism moved two starships without a conventional warp signature. Imagine what it could move with a trained operator.

**KIM**  
We don't know whether the process can be repeated safely.

**JANEWAY**  
Then we learn.

**CAVIT**  
And if the alien refuses to cooperate?

**JANEWAY**  
The Empire has centuries of experience making reluctant partners reconsider.

Harry studies the image and says nothing. Janeway notices.

**JANEWAY**  
You disagree, Ensign?

**KIM**  
I don't have enough information to disagree, Captain.

**JANEWAY**  
A wise answer. Keep collecting it.



### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — STORAGE BAY — LATER

Chakotay sits beside a crate of stolen medical supplies, checking each ampoule by hand. Suder enters carrying a broken stun baton.

**SUDER**  
This was taken from the Imperial prisoner transport. I thought you should have it.

**CHAKOTAY**  
You could have left it in the weapons locker.

**SUDER**  
I wanted to hand it to you. I don't want to keep it.

Chakotay sets down the ampoule.

**CHAKOTAY**  
What happened on the transport?

**SUDER**  
The guards surrendered. I knew they had surrendered. For a moment I wanted them to change their minds.

**CHAKOTAY**  
But they didn't.

**SUDER**  
No. I left the room before I could give them a reason.

Chakotay takes the baton.

**CHAKOTAY**  
You did the right thing.

**SUDER**  
I don't know whether that means the same thing when it's difficult.

**CHAKOTAY**  
It means more.

Tuvok appears at the doorway, having heard enough to know that neither man is performing for him.

**TUVOK**  
The Imperial patrols have shifted their search pattern. Someone has anticipated our route.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Someone, or you?

**TUVOK**  
An accusation is not a tactical plan.

**CHAKOTAY**  
No. But sometimes it's the beginning of one.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — ENGINEERING — CONTINUOUS

B'Elanna works beneath a flickering panel. Chakotay joins her. She has built a tiny model of an Imperial transponder from scrap circuitry.

**B'ELANNA**  
This is how they find us. They don't track the ship. They track the maintenance signature in our power regulators. It's a habit they taught every engineer in their fleet.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Can you change it?

**B'ELANNA**  
I can change it six times before the regulator melts. After that, we'll have to decide whether to breathe or move.

**CHAKOTAY**  
What would your mother have done?

She stops working.

**B'ELANNA**  
She'd have told me not to build my life around what the Empire took. Then she'd have stolen the regulator herself.

**CHAKOTAY**  
She sounds like someone I would have liked.

**B'ELANNA**  
She would have liked you. She would have told you to stop apologizing for giving orders.

He takes that in. He has never quite learned how to carry the people he has led to their deaths.


## ACT THREE

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — SICKBAY

The DOCTOR flickers into existence among wounded personnel.

**DOCTOR**  
Please state the nature of the medical emergency.

He surveys the room.

**DOCTOR**  
Apparently, several dozen of them. Which officer authorized this level of operational incompetence?

A nurse starts to answer. The Doctor is already working.

**DOCTOR**  
Never mind. Bring me the most salvageable patients first.

The nurse freezes at the wording.

**DOCTOR**  
That was an instruction, not a philosophical invitation.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — BRIDGE

B'Elanna repairs the main display while Chakotay checks survivors.

**CHAKOTAY**  
How many?

**B'ELANNA**  
Six dead. Three missing. The warp core is cracked. We won't make it home on this ship.

Suder looks at a flashing damage report.

**SUDER**  
Voyager's weapons are targeting us.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Hail them.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIDGE

Chakotay appears on the viewscreen.

**JANEWAY**  
Commander Chakotay. Your rebellion has had an inconvenient change of scenery.

**CHAKOTAY**  
So has your empire.

**JANEWAY**  
Lower your shields. Transfer your crew. I guarantee medical treatment and a fair hearing.

**CHAKOTAY**  
An Imperial fair hearing usually ends with an execution.

**JANEWAY**  
Then let's avoid wasting time on formalities.

She signals tactical. Voyager's weapons glow.

A powerful pulse from the alien array interrupts every system on both ships. An eerie transmission fills the bridge: an elderly voice, speaking in an unfamiliar language that the universal translator slowly renders.

**CARETAKER (V.O.)**  
So far from home. So many wounded. I did not intend this.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — READY ROOM

Janeway, Kim and Paris study a projection of the array.

**KIM**  
The displacement field is artificial. It moved us across the galaxy in seconds.

**PARIS**  
Which means it can send us back.

**JANEWAY**  
Which means it can send an Imperial fleet anywhere in the galaxy.

Harry looks up. Paris's expression changes.

**JANEWAY**  
We are not going to beg this creature for passage. We're going to take control of its machine.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — ENGINEERING

Tuvok receives a hidden coded signal from Voyager. He reads it, erases it, and speaks calmly.

**TUVOK**  
Janeway intends to capture the array.

**B'ELANNA**  
How do you know?

**TUVOK**  
Imperial doctrine. Such a device would make every resistance sanctuary vulnerable.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Then we make sure she doesn't get it.

**B'ELANNA**  
We don't have the weapons to destroy it.

**TUVOK**  
Destruction is not required. Disabling its displacement regulators would prevent immediate use.

Chakotay considers the consequences.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Find a way to shut it down. Not blow it up. There may be people depending on it.

B'Elanna nods, already planning.

### INT. CARETAKER ARRAY — OBSERVATION CHAMBER

The room resembles a cathedral assembled by a machine that has never seen a church. Far below, energy races through a lattice of crystalline conduits. Janeway walks beside a flickering projection of the CARETAKER, an ancient intelligence struggling to maintain form.

**CARETAKER**  
You are far from the place you call home.

**JANEWAY**  
You brought us here. You will send us back.

**CARETAKER**  
I have very little time.

**JANEWAY**  
Then don't waste it.

The Caretaker shows her a hologram of the underground Ocampa settlement: children gathering water, elderly citizens watching a failing power grid.

**CARETAKER**  
They depend upon me.

**JANEWAY**  
Then they should learn not to.

**CARETAKER**  
I harmed their world. Protection is my debt.

**JANEWAY**  
Debts end when the debtor dies. Your technology need not die with you.

The Caretaker's projection dims. Janeway steps closer to the core, transfixed not by its beauty but its power.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — ENGINEERING — INTERCUT

Chakotay and B'Elanna examine a stolen scan of the array. A faint encrypted packet arrives from an unregistered source. B'Elanna recognizes the structural signature: Imperial tactical formatting.

**B'ELANNA**  
Someone aboard Voyager is sending us the array's maintenance architecture.

**CHAKOTAY**  
A trap?

**B'ELANNA**  
Probably. But the data is real. The transport regulators are here. If we disrupt the control lattice, Janeway can't move her fleet through it.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Can you stop it without destroying it?

**B'ELANNA**  
I can try. There's a manual failsafe. If the Caretaker's systems are as stable as the scans suggest, the shutdown will be contained.

**CHAKOTAY**  
And if they're not?

B'Elanna holds his gaze.

**B'ELANNA**  
Then we stop the Empire anyway.

Chakotay studies the images of the Ocampa settlement. This is not an abstract calculation; there are people beneath the array.

**CHAKOTAY**  
No deliberate attack on the habitat or its power supply. We disable transport only. If it starts to cascade, you abort.

**B'ELANNA**  
Understood.

He hesitates, then authorizes the operation. The choice weighs on him immediately.



### INT. ISS VOYAGER — AUXILIARY CONTROL — AFTER DISPLACEMENT

Emergency lamps pulse over a room full of injured officers. Harry Kim has been sent to restore a failed sensor relay. He discovers a packet of information that should not exist: a fragment of the displacement event carrying two different sets of human command codes.

He routes it to an isolated terminal. One code is Imperial. The other is a modified Maquis encryption key.

**KIM**  
That isn't possible.

A damaged panel sparks. He shuts down the terminal, then realizes he has saved a copy without thinking. He looks toward the door and hides the packet in his training archive.

A wounded security officer staggers into the room.

**SECURITY OFFICER**  
Ensign. Medical help. Now.

Harry supports the officer with one arm while using the other to restore the intercom.

**KIM**  
Sickbay, this is Operations. I have one casualty in auxiliary control. Tell me what you need.

**DOCTOR (COMMS)**  
A functioning transporter would be preferable. Failing that, two arms and the ability to follow simple instructions.

Harry almost smiles. He follows the instructions.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — SICKBAY — MOMENTS LATER

The Doctor works through a triage line. A security lieutenant points at a wounded prisoner and then at an Imperial officer.

**LIEUTENANT**  
The officer first.

**DOCTOR**  
The prisoner has internal bleeding. The officer has a fractured wrist. Your proposed order is medically indefensible.

**LIEUTENANT**  
I gave you an order.

**DOCTOR**  
And I have given you a diagnosis. If you would like to compete in a different field, I suggest chess.

He treats the prisoner. A warning flashes: *PRIORITY OVERRIDE — IMPERIAL ASSET PRESERVATION*. His hand pauses against his will.

**DOCTOR**  
How peculiar.

He resets his surgical tool and continues, a fraction of a second slower. He has noticed a conflict in his own code. It is the first thing aboard Voyager he cannot explain.


## ACT FOUR

### EXT. ARRAY — SPACE

Voyager and the raider move uneasily toward the installation. Small KAZON vessels gather in the distance.

### INT. ARRAY — TRANSPORT CHAMBER

Janeway, Paris, Kim and two Imperial guards materialize in an enormous chamber filled with light and alien machinery. On another platform stand Chakotay, B'Elanna and Tuvok, transported there by the array itself.

A frail HOLOGRAPHIC CARETAKER appears.

**CARETAKER**  
You are not the ones I sought. I have little time.

**JANEWAY**  
You brought my ship here. You will send it back.

**CARETAKER**  
The mechanism is failing. I must protect the people below.

He shows them a world and an underground Ocampa settlement. Water and energy stream from the array to the planet.

**CARETAKER**  
They cannot survive without what I provide.

**JANEWAY**  
Then they should have learned to survive without you.

**CHAKOTAY**  
You sound just like the officers who occupied my homeworld.

Janeway turns sharply.

**JANEWAY**  
Commander, your presence here is an inconvenience I can correct.

**CARETAKER**  
The Kazon will seize this place when I am gone. I must ensure it cannot be used against the Ocampa.

**JANEWAY**  
Your machine belongs to whoever can hold it.

The Caretaker flickers, weakened.


### INT. CARETAKER ARRAY — ARCHIVE CHAMBER

Tuvok studies an alien memory lattice while Janeway's team searches for the transport controls. He sees a schematic of the array: a machine designed to move matter across impossible distances, but tied to the life-support network of the Ocampa beneath the planet.

He downloads only a portion of the schematic to the official channel. A second portion goes to a covert transmitter.

**JANEWAY (COMMS)**  
Commander Tuvok, report.

**TUVOK**  
The transport grid has sufficient power for another displacement. Its control architecture is unstable.

**JANEWAY (COMMS)**  
Then stabilize it.

**TUVOK**  
The array is integrated with a planetary energy system. A forced activation could terminate that system.

**JANEWAY (COMMS)**  
The planet is not my responsibility.

Tuvok studies a holographic image of thousands of sleeping Ocampa. He makes a decision and sends the hidden data packet.

### INT. MAQUIS RAIDER — ENGINEERING — INTERCUT

B'Elanna's receiver flashes with an anonymous data burst. She opens it. The schematic includes a marked weak point and a warning: *DISABLE TRANSPORT GRID ONLY — REACTOR COUPLING UNKNOWN*.

**B'ELANNA**  
Who sent this?

**CHAKOTAY**  
Can you use it?

**B'ELANNA**  
Maybe. But whoever sent it knows the difference between stopping a weapon and destroying a station.

**CHAKOTAY**  
So do you.

**B'ELANNA**  
I know the difference. I can't promise the machine does.

He watches the array through a cracked viewport.

**CHAKOTAY**  
If Janeway takes it, every occupied world becomes reachable. Every hiding place disappears.

**B'ELANNA**  
And if I touch the wrong circuit, we lose our only way home.

**CHAKOTAY**  
I'm ordering you to disable the transport grid. Not to destroy it.

**B'ELANNA**  
Then pray the universe understands your distinction.


### INT. ARRAY — SERVICE CORRIDOR

During an electrical disturbance, B'Elanna slips away with Tuvok. He identifies an access junction.

**B'ELANNA**  
You know this machine remarkably well for a man who just saw it.

**TUVOK**  
Its architecture is logical. The displacement regulator is coupled to the power-distribution manifold.

He transfers a diagnostic map to her tricorder. It includes Imperial tactical annotations.

B'Elanna stares at the data.

**B'ELANNA**  
Where did you get this?

**TUVOK**  
There is insufficient time to explain.

**B'ELANNA**  
There had better be time later.

She places a compact disruption device inside the regulator housing.

**B'ELANNA**  
This should interrupt transport capability for several hours. No detonation, no structural damage.

**TUVOK**  
Should?

**B'ELANNA**  
Engineering isn't a religion, Tuvok.

### INT. ARRAY — CENTRAL CHAMBER

Janeway sees an Imperial diagnostic signal spike on her wrist unit. Her eyes flick toward the corridor.

**JANEWAY**  
Kim. Secure the central control system. Now.

**KIM**  
Captain, the array's core is destabilizing. I can't tell if it's a natural failure or—

An alarm shrieks.

### INT. ARRAY — SERVICE CORRIDOR

B'Elanna's device pulses. The regulator goes dark. Then power reverses through a damaged coupling.

**B'ELANNA**  
No. No, that circuit wasn't on the schematic.

**TUVOK**  
The Caretaker's power system is collapsing.

**B'ELANNA**  
The shutdown is feeding the collapse!

She tries to reverse the process. A burst of energy throws her backward. Tuvok pulls her clear.

### INT. ARRAY — CENTRAL CHAMBER

The Caretaker is fading.

**CARETAKER**  
The array... cannot endure this.

**JANEWAY**  
Stabilize it!

**CARETAKER**  
I am sorry.

He disappears.

Janeway slams her communicator.

**JANEWAY**  
Voyager, transport all away teams. Prepare to take the array under tow if necessary.

**KIM**  
Captain, the structural stress is beyond tolerance. There isn't enough time.

**JANEWAY**  
There will be if you make it.

### EXT. ARRAY — SPACE

The Kazon open fire. The array's power conduits rupture, sending rings of blue-white energy into space.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — LOWER DECK — DURING THE ARRAY COLLAPSE

A corridor buckles. Harry Kim is thrown to the deck as an emergency bulkhead slams shut, separating him from a trapped technician. Fire suppression vapor fills the air.

**TECHNICIAN (COMMS)**  
Ensign, the door won't cycle!

Harry crawls to a service panel, pulls away the cover, and manually bridges two contacts. The door twitches but remains sealed.

**KIM**  
The emergency lock is tied to the main computer. I'll have to isolate it.

**TECHNICIAN**  
You'll lose the atmospheric seal on your side.

Harry checks the oxygen readout: falling. He reroutes power from a security sensor to the door actuator. The lock releases. The technician stumbles through just before the compartment vents.

**TECHNICIAN**  
You bypassed a classified security circuit.

**KIM**  
I suppose I'll have to apologize after we survive.

As they hurry away, Harry sees that the override has left him with temporary access to a protected command log. He copies a fragment of encrypted traffic—Tuvok's routing signature—without fully understanding its significance.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — READY ROOM — AFTER THE EXPLOSION

Janeway sits alone, replaying the final seconds of the array's collapse. The holographic image shows a brief energy spike, then a signature unlike the Caretaker's own failing systems.

Cavit enters.

**CAVIT**  
We've recovered fragments of the array. Engineering believes the failure began in the transport regulators.

**JANEWAY**  
A component the Maquis had reason to target.

**CAVIT**  
Shall I prepare interrogation protocols?

**JANEWAY**  
Prepare them. Do not begin without my authorization.

**CAVIT**  
Captain?

**JANEWAY**  
If I kill everyone who might have sabotaged the array, I may kill the only people who can rebuild our engines. I intend to punish the guilty, Commander. First I intend to survive.

Cavit exits. Janeway watches the dying array vanish from the projection. She has lost her route home and perhaps the most valuable prize in Imperial history. For a moment she looks almost bereaved. Then she closes the file and opens the crew roster.



### INT. ISS VOYAGER — SECURITY CONTROL — AFTER THE EXPLOSION

Cavit has gathered a list of suspects and a dozen armed officers. Janeway enters. Behind her, Harry carries a padd showing damage reports.

**CAVIT**  
We can execute the Maquis leadership before the next watch. It would restore discipline.

**JANEWAY**  
And who would repair the propulsion system?

**CAVIT**  
Our own engineers.

Harry clears his throat.

**KIM**  
Captain, Imperial engineering estimates give us eighteen days of reactor stability. The Maquis engineer's preliminary assessment gives us thirty-six hours to prevent the first cascade.

**CAVIT**  
How did you obtain her assessment?

**KIM**  
She shouted it at the guards on the way to the brig.

**JANEWAY**  
Then perhaps we should listen when our prisoners shout.

Cavit turns red. Janeway dismisses him with a glance.

**JANEWAY**  
Ensign, what do you know about the sabotage?

Harry looks at the padd. The hidden packet is there, behind a harmless icon.

**KIM**  
Not enough to accuse anyone, Captain.

**JANEWAY**  
A remarkably careful answer.

**KIM**  
I was taught to be accurate.

**JANEWAY**  
Accuracy is useful. Loyalty is necessary. Try to remember both.

She leaves. Harry remains standing until the doors close.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — ENGINEERING — NIGHT

B'Elanna is brought to the damaged engine room in restraints. She stops at the threshold, taking in a fractured coolant manifold and the rhythmic pulse of a failing core.

**B'ELANNA**  
Who designed this bypass? A committee of admirals?

An Imperial engineer starts to protest. B'Elanna points at a pressure gauge.

**B'ELANNA**  
You've got twenty minutes before that line ruptures. If it ruptures, the core will scram and you'll spend the rest of your lives orbiting this planet without heat.

**ENGINEER**  
And what do you propose?

**B'ELANNA**  
Take off the restraints.

Janeway steps into the room.

**JANEWAY**  
You have ten minutes to prove you're worth keeping alive.

**B'ELANNA**  
Then stop wasting my time.

Janeway gestures. The restraints come off. B'Elanna immediately climbs into the machinery, already issuing orders. The Imperial engineers obey before they realize they have done so.

A gauge drops out of the red.

**ENGINEER**  
Pressure stabilized.

**B'ELANNA**  
Temporarily. You'll need a full rebuild.

**JANEWAY**  
How long?

**B'ELANNA**  
With your crew? Three weeks. With mine? Four days.

Janeway studies her. She has just heard the strongest argument for keeping her enemy alive.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — OBSERVATION LOUNGE — FINAL NIGHT

Chakotay stands across from Janeway. The viewport shows a quiet planet and, beyond it, empty stars.

**CHAKOTAY**  
If you put the Maquis in separate work details, they'll assume you intend to kill them one at a time.

**JANEWAY**  
A reasonable assumption.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Then you will have a revolt before the engines are repaired.

**JANEWAY**  
Is that a threat?

**CHAKOTAY**  
It's an assessment. You have officers who know how to fight. I have people who know how to survive without supplies. We can spend what remains of this ship proving which group hates the other more, or we can make a bargain.

**JANEWAY**  
What bargain?

**CHAKOTAY**  
No executions without trial. No punishment of families or associates. My people serve under me, and I answer to you.

**JANEWAY**  
You ask a great deal for a prisoner.

**CHAKOTAY**  
I offer a great deal for a captain with no way home.

Janeway looks at the stars. She has never needed to negotiate with a rebel leader before.

**JANEWAY**  
You will serve as first officer. Torres will lead engineering. Your people will obey my orders. If you attempt to take this ship, I will end your rebellion permanently.

**CHAKOTAY**  
And if you use this ship to build another empire?

**JANEWAY**  
Then, Commander, we will have something to argue about on the way home.

She extends her hand. Chakotay takes it. Neither believes the other has surrendered.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — OPERATIONS — LATE NIGHT

Harry works alone at the empty bridge. He creates a folder titled *ENSIGN — PERSONAL*. Within it, he files the Caretaker packet, Janeway's contradictory orders, and a copy of the new crew roster.

**KIM (V.O.)**  
Personal log. First day in the Delta Quadrant. The ship is damaged, the captain has appointed a terrorist as her first officer, and the only person who seems to know what's happening is a prisoner in engineering. I have no idea whether any of us will survive. I intend to find out why.

He locks the archive. On the main screen, Voyager's new course appears: **ALPHA QUADRANT — DISTANCE UNKNOWN**.


## ACT FIVE

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIDGE

Everyone returns aboard. B'Elanna is escorted to the bridge with Chakotay and Tuvok. Janeway watches the dying array.

**JANEWAY**  
Can it still send us home?

**KIM**  
The displacement grid is offline. Main reactor is going critical.

**JANEWAY**  
Mr. Paris, move us into transporter range of the central command module.

**PARIS**  
We'd be inside the blast radius.

**JANEWAY**  
That wasn't a request.

Chakotay sees the Kazon moving closer to the planet.

**CHAKOTAY**  
If the array explodes, debris will strike the Ocampa settlement.

**JANEWAY**  
I have a warship to save, Commander. Their settlement is not my responsibility.

B'Elanna steps forward.

**B'ELANNA**  
The power feedback is irreversible. We have to pull away.

Janeway looks directly at her.

**JANEWAY**  
How do you know that?

B'Elanna says nothing. Tuvok watches both women.

**JANEWAY**  
You did something.

**CHAKOTAY**  
I gave the order. If you need someone to blame, blame me.

Janeway's face hardens.

**JANEWAY**  
I intend to.

A violent shock wave strikes Voyager. The bridge tilts. Harry's console bursts into sparks.

**PARIS**  
Captain, if we stay, there won't be a ship left to command!

Janeway looks at the array, then at the Maquis. She makes the choice she will hate for years.

**JANEWAY**  
Withdraw. Maximum impulse.

### EXT. DELTA QUADRANT — SPACE

Voyager races away. The array blossoms into a sphere of light, then shatters. A field of debris spreads across the stars. The Kazon ships scatter.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIDGE

The shock wave passes. Silence returns.

**KIM**  
The array is gone, Captain.

Janeway stares at the empty screen.

**JANEWAY**  
Seventy thousand light-years.

**PARIS**  
At maximum warp, a very long way.

**JANEWAY**  
I wasn't asking for commentary, Mr. Paris.

She turns toward Chakotay.

**JANEWAY**  
Take them to the brig.

### INT. ISS VOYAGER — BRIG

Chakotay and B'Elanna sit in separate cells. Suder stands quietly in a third. Tuvok has been taken away for an unexplained debriefing.

**B'ELANNA**  
We stopped her from taking the array.

**CHAKOTAY**  
And stranded everyone here.

**B'ELANNA**  
You gave the order.

**CHAKOTAY**  
I know.

**SUDER**  
She'll kill us when we're no longer useful.

**CHAKOTAY**  
Then we'd better stay useful.

**FADE OUT.**
