# The Delta Quadrant War

**Premise:** After homecoming, the Empire attempts a conquest via transwarp corridors.

## Synopsis

After Voyager returns, the Emperor launches Operation Dominion to seize Delta Quadrant territory and Borg infrastructure. Janeway eventually turns against the campaign, destroying a transwarp connection to keep the Borg from reaching Earth and stranding an Imperial fleet.
