# The Caretaker

**Premise:** Janeway tries to seize the array; Maquis sabotage strands Voyager.

## Synopsis

Janeway attempts to capture the Caretaker array as a strategic weapon. Chakotay orders B’Elanna to sabotage it, with intelligence covertly supplied by Tuvok. The array is destroyed by the resulting cascade, leaving the Imperial and Maquis survivors dependent on one another.
