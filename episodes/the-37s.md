# The 37’s

**Premise:** Amelia Earhart and a free human colony challenge Imperial rule.

## Synopsis

Voyager finds Amelia Earhart and a self-governing colony descended from humans abducted in 1937. Janeway tries to claim the settlement for the Empire but must negotiate after Chakotay and B’Elanna resist her order to attack. Earhart gives Janeway a compass, challenging her understanding of leadership.
